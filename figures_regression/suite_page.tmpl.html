What the v2 test suite exercises

32 single-column cases are frozen as regression baselines for the v2 bundle (exort.exe, 84 bands, HITRAN-2024). Together they cover a warming sequence of Earth-like columns under two stars, a 2-bar CO₂ Mars with and without CO₂ ice cloud, a hazy column at two haze loadings, and single minor gases. Every code change is checked against all of them. All figures below are drawn from those committed baselines.

Earth-like columns from 250 K to 360 K

Moist-adiabat columns with H₂O and 376 ppm CO₂, run under the Sun and a 3400 K blackbody at the same total insolation (S/2 = 680 W m⁻², cos z = 0.5). OLR does not depend on the star. The M-dwarf-like star puts more of its light in the near-infrared, where water vapour absorbs, so less reaches the surface and less is reflected.

Reading these

What a baseline proves

These are the model's own outputs. A change that should not move physics must reproduce all 32 to within 1e-3 (in practice exactly). They catch regressions; the H₂O line-list page compares against line-by-line models.

Spectral densities

Per-band flux divided by band width, so shapes compare across the uneven 84-band grid. Each density is checked to integrate back to the broadband flux when the data are built.

Cloud and haze inputs

Condensate and haze mass come from the input column, not from cloud or haze physics inside ExoRT. The cloud case switches on do_exo_clouds and the haze cases do_exo_haze; the haze optics are Mie spheres from Khare et al. (1984) tholin indices.
